Discover Goa

Everything you need to know for your Goa trip

About Goa

Goa is a popular coastal destination known for its beautiful beaches, Portuguese-influenced heritage, lively markets and relaxed atmosphere.

Best Time to Visit

The cooler and drier months are generally popular for sightseeing and beach activities. Monsoon months offer a greener landscape but can bring heavier rainfall.

Popular Places in Goa

Places you can explore during your trip

Baga Beach

A popular beach known for its lively atmosphere, restaurants and water activities.

Calangute Beach

One of Goa's well-known beaches with shopping, food and beach activities.

Fort Aguada

A historic fort offering beautiful views of the Arabian Sea.

What to Wear in Goa?

Choose comfortable clothing for your trip

Summer

Light and comfortable clothes are generally suitable for warm weather.

Beach

Comfortable beachwear and suitable footwear are useful for beach activities.

Monsoon

Carry light rain protection and footwear suitable for wet conditions.

Goa Travel Tips

Carry drinking water while sightseeing.

Keep Your Phone Charged

Carry a power bank for long trips.

Plan Your Route

Group nearby places together to save travel time.

Travel Responsibly

Respect local culture and keep tourist places clean.
