Explore Jaipur

Discover the Pink City of India, famous for its magnificent forts, royal palaces, colourful markets and rich Rajasthani culture.

Why Visit Jaipur?

Experience the royal heritage and colourful culture of Rajasthan.

Royal Heritage

Explore magnificent forts, palaces and historic monuments of Jaipur.

Best Time

October to March is generally considered a comfortable period to explore Jaipur.

Perfect for

History, architecture, photography, shopping and cultural experiences.

Top Places to Explore in Jaipur

Explore the most popular attractions of the Pink City.

Amber Fort

Amer, Jaipur
★★★★★

A magnificent hilltop fort known for its grand architecture, courtyards and beautiful views.

Recommended time: 2–3 hours Famous for: Architecture & views

Hawa Mahal

Badi Choupad, Jaipur
★★★★★

Jaipur's iconic Palace of Winds, famous for its unique pink façade and hundreds of small windows.

Recommended time: 1–2 hours Famous for: Photography

City Palace

Old City, Jaipur
★★★★★

A grand royal complex featuring courtyards, museums, gateways and beautiful palace buildings.

Recommended time: 2–3 hours Famous for: Royal architecture

Jantar Mantar

Jaipur City
★★★★☆

A fascinating astronomical observatory featuring large historic instruments used for measuring celestial positions.

Recommended time: 1–2 hours Famous for: Astronomy

Jal Mahal

Man Sagar Lake, Jaipur
★★★★☆

A beautiful palace appearing to float in the middle of Man Sagar Lake, especially attractive for photography.

Recommended time: 1 hour Famous for: Lake views

Nahargarh Fort

Aravalli Hills, Jaipur
★★★★★

A historic hill fort offering panoramic views of Jaipur and beautiful sunset experiences.

Recommended time: 2 hours Famous for: Sunset & city views

Jaigarh Fort

Amer, Jaipur
★★★★☆

A massive hill fort known for its impressive defensive architecture and historic significance.

Recommended time: 2 hours Famous for: Fort architecture

Albert Hall Museum

Ram Niwas Garden, Jaipur
★★★★☆

One of Rajasthan's important museums, displaying art, crafts, historical objects and cultural collections.

Recommended time: 1–2 hours Famous for: Art & culture

Discover the colourful traditions of Rajasthan.

Music & Dance

Enjoy traditional Rajasthani folk music and colourful folk performances.

Art & Handicrafts

Royal Lifestyle

Experience the architecture and traditions inspired by Jaipur's royal heritage.

Quick Travel Tips

  • Plan fort visits during the cooler part of the day.
  • Keep enough time for exploring Jaipur's local markets.
  • Try traditional Rajasthani food during your visit.
  • Carry water and comfortable footwear while sightseeing.
  • Keep your camera ready for Jaipur's colourful architecture.
